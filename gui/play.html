Q is the model's expected result for this hand, in points (the Result column of a match: points won minus points paid, minus the dealer cost if you pay it), against three opponents drawn from the population it was trained against (pop 0: the league of its own past versions, greedy and indep). Q no dealer = Q + c × Dealer. MC is the same expectation measured by Monte Carlo against three copies of the chosen opponent. Probabilities are per opponent (the net's estimate of the fraction of the three opponents): Scoop = I scoop them, Scooped = they scoop me, Opp HR = they scoop all three of us. Dealer = my expected share of the dealer cost. Win F / M / B: my front, middle, back beats theirs (losses and ties are under Selected). Q is composed from these by the rules: under family, Q = 3 Scoop − 3 Scooped + 3 HR − 3 Opp HR + 3 bonus − c Dealer − opp bonus; with row points, also 3 × row × (wins − losses) per row; royalties paid only on a won row use the Win columns.
