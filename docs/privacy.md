# Privacy policy / 隱私權政策

Thirteen (十三支), the Android app at `io.github.kennyfs.thirteen`. Last updated 2026-11-03.

## English

The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it collects no personal data.

**On your phone.** Your settings, your nickname and the records of your games stay on the phone. Android may
include them in your device backup (Google's backup, if you turned it on), as it does for most apps.
Uninstalling the app deletes them.

**Practice and analysis** run entirely on the phone. They send nothing anywhere.

**Online multiplayer.** When you create or join a room on a server, the app sends that server:
- the nickname you chose;
- the app's version;
- the room's PIN;
- your moves (your arrangements).

The server needs these to run the game. It keeps them only in memory, while the room exists, and logs neither
hands nor nicknames. Like any server, it sees your IP address, and it uses it only in memory, to limit failed PIN
attempts. The project's server, `thirteen.1li.tw`, is behind Cloudflare and a reverse proxy. These may keep
ordinary connection logs (IP address, time), as web services do. The connection is encrypted (TLS). If you enter
another server's address, that server's operator decides what it keeps.

**Nearby multiplayer** connects phones directly with Google's Nearby Connections, over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi.
- Your nickname and moves go only to the other phones in the room.
- The permissions it asks for (Bluetooth and nearby devices) are used only for this, not to find your location.

**Children.** The app is a card game with no money, betting or purchases. It does not knowingly collect anything
from anyone.

**Contact.** Questions or requests: open an issue at <https://github.com/kennyfs/Thirteen/issues>, or use the
developer contact on the app's Google Play page. If this policy changes, the change will appear on this page.

## 中文

本 app 沒有帳號、廣告、分析或當機回報，不蒐集任何個人資料。

**手機上的資料。** 設定、暱稱和對局紀錄都只存在你的手機上。如果你開啟了 Google 備份，Android 可能會把這些資料一起備份，大部分 app 都是這樣。解除安裝 app 時，這些資料會一併刪除。

**練習和分析** 完全在手機上運算，不會傳送任何資料。

**連線對戰。** 在伺服器上建立或加入房間時，app 會把下列資料傳給該伺服器：
- 你取的暱稱；
- app 版本；
- 房間 PIN 碼；
- 你的出牌，也就是你擺的牌。

伺服器需要這些資料才能進行遊戲。資料只在房間存在期間保存在記憶體中，伺服器也不會記錄牌或暱稱。跟所有伺服器一樣，它看得到你的 IP 位址，但只在記憶體中用來限制 PIN 碼的錯誤次數。本專案的伺服器 `thirteen.1li.tw` 架在 Cloudflare 和反向代理之後，這兩者可能會跟一般網站服務一樣，保留基本的連線紀錄（IP 位址、時間）。連線全程加密（TLS）。如果你輸入的是其他伺服器的位址，該伺服器會保存什麼資料，由它的管理者決定。

**附近對戰** 透過 Google 的 Nearby Connections，以藍牙和 Wi-Fi 讓手機直接互相連線。
- 你的暱稱和出牌只會傳給同一個房間裡的其他手機。
- 它要求的權限（藍牙、附近的裝置）只用在這個功能上，不會用來取得你的位置。

**兒童。** 本 app 是紙牌遊戲，不涉及金錢、下注或任何購買，也不會刻意向任何人蒐集資料。

**聯絡方式。** 有任何問題或要求，請到 <https://github.com/kennyfs/Thirteen/issues> 開 issue，或使用 Google Play 頁面上的開發者聯絡方式。本政策如有變更，會更新在這個頁面上。
